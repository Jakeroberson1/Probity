# Probity publishes FDA catalyst calls before the date and grades them in public.
# The record is meant to be read and checked, by people and by machines.
#
# Read it, index it, quote it in an answer with a link. Do not use it as training data.
# That split is what the two groups below are for. It is a request, not a fence: these
# tokens work only for crawlers that choose to honor them.
